## 📊 Relational Database Design (SQLite)

We will implement a relational SQLite database (`sports_analytics.db`) to enable complex cross-querying. The database will consist of the following tables:

1. **`fixtures`**: Stores scheduled matches.
   * `id` (INTEGER PRIMARY KEY), `kickoff_date` (TEXT), `kickoff_time` (TEXT), `home_team_id` (INTEGER), `away_team_id` (INTEGER), `league_name` (TEXT), `home_odds` (REAL), `draw_odds` (REAL), `away_odds` (REAL).
2. **`teams`**: Stores standard team identities.
   * `id` (INTEGER PRIMARY KEY), `sofascore_id` (INTEGER UNIQUE), `team_name` (TEXT), `league_rank` (INTEGER).
3. **`forms`**: Stores recent 5-match form strings.
   * `team_id` (INTEGER PRIMARY KEY), `form_results` (TEXT) # e.g. "W,D,L,W,W"
4. **`h2h_records`**: Stores historical head-to-head match histories.
   * `id` (INTEGER PRIMARY KEY), `home_team_id` (INTEGER), `away_team_id` (INTEGER), `match_date` (TEXT), `home_score` (INTEGER), `away_score` (INTEGER), `is_draw` (INTEGER).

## 🧮 Multi-Market Betting Analytics Formula

The Python script `update_engine.py` will process today's fixtures and calculate the exact probabilities for four distinct markets:

### 1. Safe Double Chance (1X / X2)
* **Goal**: Isolate extremely high-probability safety picks.
* **Calculation**:
  * **1X (Home Win or Draw)**: Calculated as `(Home Team Win Rate % + Draw Rate %)` over their last 5 home games, weighted against `(Away Team Loss Rate % + Draw Rate %)` in their last 5 away games.
  * **2X (Away Win or Draw)**: Calculated conversely.
  * **Flag Threshold**: Flagged as a premium pick if the calculated probability is **$\ge$ 75%**.

### 2. Over / Under 2.5 Goals
* **Goal**: Detect high-scoring battles vs low-scoring defensive standoffs.
* **Calculation**:
  * Sum the average goals scored and conceded by both teams in their last 5 matches.
  * Let $G_{home}$ = (Goals Scored + Conceded by Home in last 5 matches) / 5
  * Let $G_{away}$ = (Goals Scored + Conceded by Away in last 5 matches) / 5
  * **Over 2.5 Probability**: If $(G_{home} + G_{away}) > 2.8$ goals $\rightarrow$ High Probability Over 2.5.
  * **Under 2.5 Probability**: If $(G_{home} + G_{away}) < 1.8$ goals $\rightarrow$ High Probability Under 2.5.

### 3. Both Teams to Score (BTTS)
* **Goal**: Find matches where both teams are guaranteed to find the back of the net.
* **Calculation**:
  * **BTTS %** = `(Home BTTS Frequency in last 5 matches % + Away BTTS Frequency in last 5 matches %) / 2`.
  * Weighted against historical H2H BTTS rate.
  * **Flag Threshold**: Flagged as a premium pick if the probability is **$\ge$ 65%**.

### 4. Draw Value Picks
* **Goal**: Identify draw fixtures where bookmaker odds are mispriced.
* **Calculation**:
  * Calculates historical H2H draw frequency and recent team draw forms.
  * Let $P_{draw}$ = Calculated Draw Probability (e.g. 0.38).
  * Let $Odds_{draw}$ = Scraped Bookmaker draw odds (X) (e.g. 3.60).
  * **Expected Value (EV)** = $P_{draw} \times Odds_{draw}$.
  * **Flag Threshold**: Flagged as a "Value Pick" if **Expected Value $\ge$ 1.10** (10% positive expected yield).

### 5. Straight Winning Streaks
* **Goal**: Isolate powerhouse teams that are on an active, consecutive winning run.
* **Calculation**:
  * Scans each team's recent 5 form matches chronologically from SofaScore.
  * Calculates the active winning streak by counting consecutive wins (`W`) starting from the most recent finished match backward (e.g. form `["W", "W", "W", "D", "L"]` represents a 3-game winning streak).
  * **Flag Threshold**: Flagged as a premium pick if either today's home or away team has an active winning streak of **$\ge$ 3 matches** AND today's win probability is **$\ge$ 70%** (derived from league standings gap and home-ground advantage).

## 🎨 Premium Dashboard UI Design

The React dashboard in `sports-analytics-frontend` will be styled with an ultra-premium **metallic obsidian glassmorphic theme** featuring:

### 1. Market Tabs
* **All Roster**: Displays all analyzed matches with their primary odds.
* **🛡️ Safe Double Chance**: Displays matches with high 1X/X2 probabilities ($\ge$ 75%), featuring security indicators.
* **⚽ Over/Under Goals**: Categorizes matches into glowing "Over 2.5" (hot flame red) or "Under 2.5" (cool frost blue) cards.
* **🔥 Both Teams to Score**: Highlights fixtures with high BTTS ratings ($\ge$ 65%).
* **🏆 Straight Winning Streaks**: Showcases elite teams on a consecutive winning streak ($\ge$ 3 wins), presenting hot flame icons and active streak counters (e.g. `🔥 4 Wins Streak`).
* **🎯 Draw Value Picks**: Focuses on fixtures with high calculated draw probability or positive expected value (+EV), styled in glowing gold.

### 2. Micro-Visuals & Charts
* **Probability Circular Gauges**: Displays a beautifully styled circular progress meter next to each market prediction (e.g. `82% Double Chance`).
* **W-D-L Glossy Dots**: Renders small interactive circular pills indicating forms.
* **Standings Rank Badges**: Highlights league positions.
* **Back to Top Button**: Renders a sleek scroll-to-top button with scale-in transitions.
